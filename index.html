Our System

Precision-engineered light gauge steel frame construction — factory-controlled, site-assembled, and built to outperform traditional methods in every measurable way.

The System

Light Gauge Steel
Frame Construction

Cold-Formed Sections

Galvanised steel coil is roll-formed into C and U profiles between 0.8 mm and 2.5 mm gauge. Every stud, track, joist and truss member is cut, punched and service-holed on the line — no cutting or drilling on site.

Design to Machine

The structural model drives the roll-former directly. Panel geometry, screw patterns and part labelling come out of the same file the engineer signs off, so what is approved is exactly what is manufactured.

Panelised Delivery

Walls, floor cassettes and roof trusses arrive as pre-panelised assemblies, numbered to the erection drawing. A typical house shell is stood up and weathertight in days rather than weeks.

Durability

Steel does not rot, warp, shrink or burn, and is unaffected by insect attack. Galvanised coating protects the frame over its design life, and the steel is recyclable at end of life.

Loads & Climate

The same system is engineered for Canadian snow loads, Mediterranean seismic zones and UK wind exposure — the profile gauge and bracing change, the method does not.

Applications

Load-bearing structures, SFS infill to hot-rolled or concrete frames, roof extensions and loft conversions, and hybrid schemes where light steel sits on a hot-rolled transfer structure.

The Vision

Founded on a Mission
to Build Better

EcoHome was founded in 2017 with a conviction that the built environment should be both beautiful and enduring — not a compromise between the two.

The goal was to make sustainable, steel-framed construction the standard in the UK, not the exception. Having seen first-hand how poor-quality housing created long-term social and environmental costs, EcoHome set out to prove that precision-engineered homes could look exactly like — and outperform — traditional construction in every meaningful way.

“I spent years on construction sites seeing the same compromise repeated: homes taking longer to build than they should, with quality too dependent on weather, labour and site conditions. The engineering already existed to do it better. EcoHome was created to bring that precision into housing.”

Walid — Founder & Civil Engineer

But better engineering was only half the ambition.

We wanted to prove that modern construction doesn’t have to look industrial or feel temporary. EcoHome homes are designed to be architecturally considered, warm, solid and beautifully finished — homes people genuinely want to live in, not simply homes that are faster to build.

Built with precision. Designed with purpose. Made to endure.
